# Podcast clip
Use for: 30 to 90 s clips of speech, quotes, interviews. Needs clear audio.
Avoid when: the visuals themselves must tell the story (screen demos).
Layout: name tag lower-left, large karaoke captions centre-bottom, thin progress bar at the very bottom, subtle waveform near the captions; keep the speaker's face clear.
