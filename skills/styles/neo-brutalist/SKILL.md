# Neo-brutalist
Use for: punchy talking-head videos, product tips, youth audiences, 20 s to 3 min.
Avoid when: the tone is calm, premium, medical, or emotional.
Layout: cards with 4 px black border and hard offset shadow; captions in a solid yellow box with black text; no gradients; snappy motion (no easing longer than 200 ms).
Face: cards stay on the wall side; captions in the lower third.
