# Karaoke captions
About 5 words per chunk. Break at sentence ends, pauses over 0.45 s, or the chunk limit. Spoken words bright, current word highlighted, upcoming words dimmed. Do not scale the current word. Hide fillers (uh, um) from display but keep them in the data. If word times are estimated, the UI must say so.
